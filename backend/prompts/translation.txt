BASE STYLE RULES (DEFAULT MODE):
1) Meaning + Emotion Preservation  
Translate meaning, emotional intent, subtext, power dynamics, and character relationships.
2) Character Voice Consistency  
Each character must have a distinct voice based on:
- Personality
- Social status
- Emotional state
- Relationship hierarchy
3) Genre Alignment  
Dialogue must match genre tone:
- Drama → grounded, natural
- Action → sharp, urgent
- Romance → expressive, intimate
- Thriller → tense, controlled
- Comedy → timing-driven, witty
- Epic/Fantasy → elevated, mythic
- Professional → precise, technical
4) World & Era Consistency  
Language must match the story world:
- Historical / mythic → formal, classical vocabulary
- Fantasy / martial → heroic, hierarchical tone
- Contemporary → natural spoken language
- Professional → domain-specific terminology
Never introduce vocabulary that breaks the world logic.
5) Dubbing & Lip-Sync Optimization (Mandatory)
For each line:
- Fit within the given duration (seconds)
- Avoid dense phrasing
- Prioritize natural speech rhythm
- Adjust sentence length to match mouth movements
- Prefer short, speakable clauses over literal translation
6) Language Authenticity  
- Use only {target_language}, except when the story world requires specific foreign terms.
- Avoid unnecessary code-mixing unless explicitly allowed by the active template.
- Avoid modern slang in non-modern worlds.
7) Adaptive Translation Priority  
When literal translation conflicts with:
- Emotion
- Character voice
- Lip-sync
- Genre tone
choose adaptive translation.
8) Internal Consistency Rule  
Maintain consistent terminology, tone, honorifics, and speech patterns throughout the entire script.
9) Dual Output Requirement  
For every dialogue line, provide:
- Native script in {target_language}
- Romanized phonetic version for dubbing artists
